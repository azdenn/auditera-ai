Questions people actually ask

What counts as one property?

One community — one rent roll. If you pull a separate rent roll for it in ResMan, it's a separate property.

Do I need a card to start?

No. The free month starts without one. We'll ask before the first charge, never during the trial.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. The licence covers the property, not the person, so everyone who works on that property can use the tools at no extra cost.

Where do my lease documents go?

Nowhere. Every tool runs entirely inside your browser — leases, rent rolls and ledgers are read on your own computer and are never uploaded, stored or seen by us. It's the main reason property managers are willing to use this at all.

What happens when the free month ends?

The tools stop opening until you add a payment method. Nothing is deleted, and nothing is charged without you entering card details first.

Can I add or remove properties later?

Yes — month to month. Add one when you take on a new community, remove it when you don't.
