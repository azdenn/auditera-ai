What it does

Purpose-built tools for the records that matter most

Auditera AI gives multifamily teams purpose-built tools for checking the records that matter most — without manually comparing documents line by line.

Compare executed leases against your property records and quickly identify discrepancies. Auditera AI reads the executed lease, compares its critical terms against the Rent Roll, and clearly flags anything that doesn't match.

  • Rent & recurring charges — matched line by line against the lease
  • Resident & unit details — names and unit numbers checked against the Rent Roll
  • Deposits & Lease Lock — traditional deposits and Lease Lock programs verified for consistency
  • Lease dates & term — start date, end date, and actual term read from the signed lease, never assumed
  • Required signatures — checked across the full lease packet, not just the last page
  • Exportable results — download the discrepancy list for follow-up

Work out exactly how many weeks of free rent each resident is actually getting, and whether that matches the deal on the lease — instead of scanning months of ledger activity by hand.

  • Free rent taken up front — adds up every move-in special credit on the ledger, because a six-week special is often split across two months
  • Free rent spread over the lease — runs the rent roll's recurring credit out over the term and flags any that doesn't land on a whole number of weeks
  • The lease is the source of truth — the rent and term come from the signed lease, and anywhere the rent roll disagrees is reported on its own
  • Exportable results — download the problem list for follow-up
