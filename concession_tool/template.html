ConcessionVerify — Auditera AI

Checks two things for every unit: (1) if a resident got a move-in discount (a "concession"), were they credited the right amount, and (2) is everyone being billed the correct rent and fees. Just upload your files below and click one button — everything else happens automatically.

1 Upload your files

Upload all three files below, then click Process. This tool checks concessions only — move-in specials and prorated free rent. All three are required, including the leases: the lease is the binding document, so its rent and its term are what every week of free rent is measured against. Nothing is uploaded to the internet; it all stays on this page.
